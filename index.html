Six thousand km of coast, desert and monsoon forest — one country best seen slowly.

From the marble inlay of Agra to the backwaters of Kerala and the shorelines of Goa, this guide covers where to go and what to expect when you get there.

See the destinations

Three places to start with

Agra, Uttar Pradesh

Taj Mahal

Arrive at the east gate before sunrise. The marble changes colour through the morning, and the crowds arrive by mid-day.

Best months: Oct – Mar

Kerala

Backwaters

A network of lagoons and canals behind the Malabar coast, best explored slowly on a converted rice barge.

Best months: Sep – Mar

Goa

Beaches

Portuguese-era churches and spice markets sit inland, minutes from a coastline that runs from Baga to Palolem.

Best months: Nov – Feb

Jaipur, Rajasthan

The Pink City

Fort palaces, bazaars selling block-printed textiles, and sandstone facades that turn rose-coloured at sunset.

Best months: Oct – Mar

Varanasi

Ghats of the Ganges

One of the world's oldest living cities. Boats leave before dawn for the view of the river ghats from the water.

Best months: Oct – Mar

Ladakh

High desert

Buddhist monasteries above 3,000 metres, reached by mountain roads that only open a few months a year.

Best months: Jun – Sep
